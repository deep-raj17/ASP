# Re-entry Conditions

Research may resume only when at least one documented condition occurs:

1. Official MIMII archives become downloadable and all twelve are verified.
2. An alternative official mirror becomes available and is authenticated.
3. The publisher provides verified access or acquisition evidence.
4. A previously completed verified acquisition becomes available from an
   authorized source.
5. Equivalent official evidence is obtained through documented institutional
   channels.

After re-entry: authenticate source, verify MD5, extract verified archives,
perform file-level SHA-256 comparison with `E:\MIMII`, reassess PMPS-01, and
continue to PMPS-02 only if PMPS-01 is resolved.

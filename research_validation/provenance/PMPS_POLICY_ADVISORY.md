# PMPS Policy Advisory

PMPS-01’s `dataset_license_identity` requirement is consistent with reasonable
scientific provenance because authoritative archive identity evidence exists
for this release. It is not an impossible requirement and should not be
silently weakened to a disclosure-only limitation.

Retain the current blocking rule until local archive/acquisition linkage is
verified. Any conditional policy would require a formal versioned governance
decision, not an ad hoc gate relaxation.

# Dataset License Identity Certification

**Decision: BLOCKED**

The canonical source identified by repository evidence is the MIMII dataset,
public 1.0, published through the official Zenodo record
<https://zenodo.org/records/3384388>, with a CC BY-SA 4.0 license recorded in
the provenance report. This establishes a credible source and license record
for the cited release.

The official record does publish MD5 checksums for the twelve archive ZIPs.
However, the local extracted directory has no preserved acquisition receipt or
original archive available for comparison. Directory structure and file counts
agree, but structural agreement is not cryptographic identity. Therefore local
license identity and publication permissions for the exact extracted files
remain **BLOCKED**. The local evidence is now **CONFLICTING**: one present
archive mismatches the official MD5 and three official archives are missing.

Exactly one closure action remains: resolve the MD5 mismatch and recover the
three missing official archives or equivalent authoritative acquisition
evidence.

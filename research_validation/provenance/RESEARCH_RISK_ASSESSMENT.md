# Research Risk Assessment

| Risk | Level | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Reproducibility | High | Exact historical archive lineage is unresolved |
| Scientific validity | Moderate | Extracted files pass integrity checks; provenance conflict remains |
| Dataset identity | High | One MD5 mismatch and three unavailable archives |
| Licensing documentation | Moderate | Official license known, local-byte linkage unverified |
| Publication transparency | High | A complete provenance certificate cannot be issued |

The risks do not demonstrate that historical results are invalid; they limit
what can be claimed about dataset identity and exact reproducibility.

# Publication Disclosure Guidance

Do not state that the local corpus is fully cryptographically verified against
MIMII Public 1.0. Recommended disclosure:

> The study used a locally extracted MIMII corpus whose structure, manifest
> composition, file readability, finite-value checks, metadata, and current-file
> hashes were audited. Official archive MD5 comparison matched five available
> containers; one available container differed and three archive containers were
> unavailable. A fresh reference acquisition was attempted but blocked by
> repeated transfer timeouts. Consequently, exact archive-level equivalence of
> the historical corpus to the official release could not be established.

Any manuscript using the historical results must include this limitation and
avoid claims of fully verified dataset provenance.

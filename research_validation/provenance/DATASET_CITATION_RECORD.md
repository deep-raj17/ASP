# Dataset Citation Record

Canonical source: MIMII (Malfunctioning Industrial Machine Investigation and
Inspection), public 1.0, official Zenodo record:
<https://zenodo.org/records/3384388>.

The project must cite the official record and preserve its attribution and
share-alike notice when distributing permitted derived artifacts. This citation
does not by itself prove that the local extracted files came from that archive;
the official record's twelve archive MD5 values are recorded in
`DATASET_HASH_REGISTRY.csv`, but local comparison remains the single PMPS-01
blocker.
